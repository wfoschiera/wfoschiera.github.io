About

I'm a software engineer at Jusbrasil, working on Jus IA, an AI assistant for legal professionals. I build product features end to end in TypeScript and Python, and measure them with SQL and A/B tests.

I came to software the long way. I studied mathematics, did a master's in remote sensing at INPE, and spent 15 years in public service: leading field-survey teams, validating the data they collected and, from 2015, mapping municipal road networks. Along the way I wrote QGIS plugins in Python, and at the end of 2021 I moved to software full time, starting with Django and Vue.js.

Outside work I run a small homelab, keep an eye on geospatial tooling, and practice on NeetCode, where I've solved more than 250 LeetCode-style problems, mostly data structures and algorithms, plus SQL.

Research

Sugarcane crop yield estimation based on MODIS and official statistics data association MSc dissertation in Remote Sensing · INPE, 2015

Using MODIS NDVI time series as proxies, I estimated sugarcane yield for 361 municipalities in São Paulo state. In most regions the estimates came within 5% of official statistics, and the method can run during the season, while official figures come out a year or two after harvest.
